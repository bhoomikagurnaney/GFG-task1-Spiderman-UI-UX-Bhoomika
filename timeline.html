20+ Years On Screen

The Spider-Man Movie Timeline

From Tobey Maguire's first swing in 2002 to Tom Holland's Brand New Day, every live-action and animated chapter, in order.

2002

Spider-Man

Peter Parker gets bitten by a genetically modified spider and learns what it means to be a hero, starring Tobey Maguire.

2004

Spider-Man 2

Peter struggles to balance his personal life and his responsibilities as he faces Doctor Octopus.

2007

Spider-Man 3

An alien symbiote amplifies Peter's darker side as he faces Sandman, Venom, and the New Goblin.

2012

The Amazing Spider-Man

Andrew Garfield takes on the mask in a rebooted origin story that digs into Peter's parents' disappearance.

2014

The Amazing Spider-Man 2

Peter faces Electro and the Green Goblin while trying to protect the people he loves most.

2017

Spider-Man: Homecoming

Tom Holland's Peter Parker balances high school life with his first steps as an Avenger, mentored by Tony Stark.

2018

Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse

Miles Morales discovers he's one of many Spider-People across the multiverse in this animated film.

2019

Spider-Man: Far From Home

Peter takes on the mantle of a global hero and faces Mysterio while on a school trip to Europe.

2021

Spider-Man: No Way Home

A spell gone wrong tears open the multiverse, bringing villains and Spider-Men from other timelines into Peter's world.

2023

Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse

Miles Morales journeys across the multiverse and collides with a society of Spider-People over its sacred rules.

2026

Spider-Man: Brand New Day

Forgotten by the world but not by himself, Peter Parker rebuilds his life as Spider-Man from the ground up.
